# Park

## Description

```text
There's a park near my apartment.
It's about ten minutes away on foot, so it is very easy to get there.
It is not huge, but it has a walking trail, a playground, and some open space.
I usually go there with my family on weekends.
I like it because it gives us a simple way to relax without planning anything special.
```

## Past Experience

```text
Last weekend, I went to a park with my family.
We were walking around the trail when my son suddenly saw a big dog.
He was really excited and ran toward it, so I was a little worried at first because the dog was much bigger than him.
Fortunately, the dog was very calm, and the owner was kind enough to let my son pet it.
After that, my son kept talking about the dog for the rest of the day.
What I remember most is that a very small moment made the whole afternoon special.
```
