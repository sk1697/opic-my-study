# Technology

## Routine

```text
I use my smartphone all the time.
In the morning, I check messages and my schedule.
During the day, I use it for work-related communication and quick searches.
At night, I usually watch short videos or read news.
It is useful, but I try not to use it too much before going to bed.
```

## Past Experience

```text
Last month, I had a problem with my smartphone while I was outside.
I was using a map app to find a restaurant when the battery suddenly died.
At first, I was frustrated because I did not know the exact location.
So I went into a nearby cafe and asked if I could charge my phone for a few minutes.
Fortunately, the staff was very helpful, and I was able to turn my phone back on.
In the end, I found the restaurant, but the experience reminded me to carry a small charger.
```
