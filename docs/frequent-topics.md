# 고빈도 주제별 3문제

이 페이지는 지금까지 대화에서 나온 목표와 약점을 기준으로 만들었습니다. 공식 출제 빈도를 보장하는 목록은 아니고, **IH 안정화에 자주 쓰이는 주제**와 **사용자가 이미 준비한 Park, Family, Work, Technology 흐름**을 확장한 학습자료입니다.

각 주제는 3문제만 반복하세요.

1. **Description**: 장소, 사람, 물건을 설명합니다.
2. **Routine**: 평소에 무엇을 하는지 말합니다.
3. **Past Experience**: 기억나는 사건을 60초 이야기로 만듭니다.

## 공통 IH 답변 공식

```text
Description:
The place/person/thing I want to talk about is...
What I like most is...
I usually...
That's why it is important to me.

Routine:
I usually do this when...
First, I...
After that, I...
It helps me...

Past Experience:
A few weeks ago...
I was... when suddenly...
At first, I felt...
So I...
Fortunately...
In the end...
What I remember most is...
```

## 1. Park & Walking

**메모리 포인트:** Park는 주말, 산책, 가족, 아이, 날씨, 작은 사건을 연결하기 쉽습니다. 외운 표현보다 “평소 산책하다가 생긴 일”로 말하면 IH에 필요한 흐름이 잘 나옵니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Describe a park you often go to.
2. What do you usually do at the park?
3. Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** near my home → quiet path → trees → benches → good for relaxing
- **Routine:** weekend → walk with family → talk about small things → feel refreshed
- **Experience:** sunny afternoon → child/dog/noise → surprised → handled calmly → warm memory

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
A few weeks ago, I went to a park near my home with my family.
We were walking slowly and talking about our week when suddenly a small dog ran toward us.
At first, I was worried because my child was surprised.
But the dog was actually friendly, so I smiled and talked to the owner.
After that, my family relaxed again, and we sat on a bench for a while.
In the end, it was not a big event, but it became a warm memory because we laughed about it together.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

단순히 “I like the park. It is clean. I walk there.”로 끝내지 말고, **언제 갔는지, 갑자기 무슨 일이 생겼는지, 내가 어떻게 반응했는지**를 넣으세요.

## 2. Family

**메모리 포인트:** Family는 감정 표현이 자연스럽게 들어갑니다. 긴 설명보다 “계획이 바뀐 날” 하나를 준비하면 여러 질문에 돌려 쓸 수 있습니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Tell me about your family.
2. What do you usually do with your family?
3. Tell me about an unexpected experience you had with your family.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** small family → comfortable → honest conversations → supportive
- **Routine:** dinner/weekend → simple food → movie/talk → recharge
- **Experience:** plan changed → rain/crowd/problem → disappointed → changed plan → better memory

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
One weekend, my family planned to go outside, but it suddenly started raining heavily.
At first, we were disappointed because we had been looking forward to it.
So we changed the plan and made a simple meal at home.
After dinner, we watched a movie and talked for a long time.
Surprisingly, the day felt more comfortable than our original plan.
What I remember most is that a small problem helped us spend more time together.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

가족 소개만 길게 하지 말고, **작은 문제 → 감정 → 대안 → 결과**를 넣어 이야기를 완성하세요.

## 3. Work & Office

**메모리 포인트:** Work는 실수, 회의, 파일 문제, 동료 도움을 넣으면 자연스럽습니다. 직장 이야기는 정확한 직무 설명보다 “문제를 해결한 경험”이 점수에 더 유리합니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Describe your workplace.
2. What is your normal work routine?
3. Tell me about a problem you had at work.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** office/team → computer work → meetings → practical atmosphere
- **Routine:** check messages → organize tasks → work on files → talk with coworkers
- **Experience:** file issue/deadline → nervous → backup/help → solved → habit changed

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
A few months ago, I had an important meeting at work.
I was preparing the final file when suddenly I noticed that one section was missing.
At first, I felt nervous because the meeting was about to start.
So I checked my backup folder and asked a coworker to review the numbers with me.
Fortunately, we fixed the file before the meeting.
Since then, I always save an extra copy before important work.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

업무 루틴을 나열하지 말고, **압박감이 있었던 상황**과 **그 후 습관이 어떻게 바뀌었는지**까지 말하세요.

## 4. Technology & Smartphone

**메모리 포인트:** 스마트폰 문제는 OPIc에서 매우 쓰기 좋은 경험형 소재입니다. 배터리, 지도 앱, 인터넷 연결, 사진 저장 문제 중 하나만 확실히 준비하세요.

### 3 Questions

1. What technology do you use most often?
2. How do you usually use your smartphone?
3. Tell me about a problem you had while using a smartphone.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** smartphone → useful → map, messages, photos, banking
- **Routine:** morning messages → work calls → map app → short breaks
- **Experience:** battery died/lost signal → frustrated → found cafe/asked someone → bought charger → lesson

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
Last month, I was going to a place I had never visited before.
I was using a map app on my phone when suddenly the battery went down to one percent.
At first, I felt frustrated because I did not know the area well.
So I went into a nearby cafe and asked if I could charge my phone for a few minutes.
Luckily, the staff was kind, and I found the address again.
After that experience, I started carrying a small charger in my bag.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

기술 설명에서 멈추지 말고, **내 생활에서 왜 중요한지**와 **문제가 생겼을 때 어떻게 해결했는지**를 연결하세요.

## 5. Cafe & Restaurant

**메모리 포인트:** Cafe는 친구, 가족, 기다림, 주문 실수, 분위기 설명을 넣기 좋습니다. Park와 Family 소재를 섞어도 자연스럽습니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Describe a cafe or restaurant you like.
2. What do you usually do there?
3. Tell me about something interesting that happened at a cafe or restaurant.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** near home/work → quiet or lively → comfortable seats → good coffee/food
- **Routine:** meet someone → order the same drink → talk or work → relax
- **Experience:** crowded cafe/order mistake/lost item → confused → staff helped → positive ending

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
There is a small cafe near my home, and I often go there on weekends.
One day, I ordered coffee and sat with my family when the staff brought the wrong drink.
At first, I was a little confused, but the staff apologized right away.
They changed the drink and even gave us a small dessert.
The situation was simple, but it made the cafe feel warmer and more personal.
Since then, I remember that place not just for the coffee but for the kind service.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

맛있다, 좋다만 반복하지 말고, **공간 분위기와 사람의 반응**을 같이 말하세요.

## 6. Travel & Trip

**메모리 포인트:** Travel은 길 찾기, 날씨, 예약 문제, 기억나는 장소로 확장하기 좋습니다. 완벽한 여행보다 작은 문제가 있는 여행이 더 말하기 쉽습니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Describe a place you traveled to.
2. What do you usually do when you travel?
3. Tell me about a memorable trip.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** city/place → first impression → food/view/people → different from daily life
- **Routine:** search places → take photos → try local food → walk around
- **Experience:** weather/transportation problem → worried → changed schedule → discovered better place

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
Last year, I took a short trip with my family.
We planned to visit a famous outdoor place, but it started raining in the morning.
At first, I was disappointed because I had already checked the route.
So we changed our plan and went to a small indoor market instead.
It turned out to be a better choice because we tried local food and talked with shop owners.
In the end, the trip was memorable because the unexpected change made it more natural.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

장소 소개만 하지 말고, **예상과 달랐던 점**을 넣으면 이야기성이 생깁니다.

## 7. Home & Neighborhood

**메모리 포인트:** 집과 동네는 거의 모든 주제에 연결됩니다. Park, Cafe, Shopping 답변의 시작점을 “near my home”으로 고정하면 답변 준비가 쉬워집니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Describe your home or neighborhood.
2. What do you usually do near your home?
3. Tell me about a change in your neighborhood.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** quiet area → convenient stores → park/cafe nearby → comfortable
- **Routine:** walk → buy groceries → visit cafe → spend time with family
- **Experience:** new store/road construction/noise → inconvenient at first → got used to it → improved life

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
My neighborhood is quiet, but it is also convenient.
There is a park, a cafe, and a few small stores near my home.
Recently, a new store opened on the main street.
At first, the construction was noisy, so I did not like it.
But after it opened, I realized it was useful because I could buy things quickly after work.
Now I feel my neighborhood is more convenient than before.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

동네 묘사는 쉬워 보이지만 짧아지기 쉽습니다. **변화 전후 비교**를 넣어 길이를 확보하세요.

## 8. Shopping

**메모리 포인트:** Shopping은 온라인 주문, 사이즈 문제, 배송 지연, 환불 경험으로 말하기 좋습니다. Technology 답변과 연결하기 쉽습니다.

### 3 Questions

1. Where do you usually shop?
2. What do you usually buy?
3. Tell me about a problem you had while shopping.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** online/offline store → convenient → compare prices → read reviews
- **Routine:** search → check reviews → compare price → order or visit store
- **Experience:** wrong size/delayed delivery/payment issue → annoyed → contacted service → solved → learned

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
I usually shop online because it saves time.
One time, I ordered a jacket after reading many reviews.
But when it arrived, the size was much smaller than I expected.
At first, I was annoyed because I needed it for the weekend.
So I contacted customer service and exchanged it for a larger size.
The process took a few days, but it taught me to check the size chart more carefully.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

쇼핑 습관만 말하지 말고, **문제가 생긴 주문 하나**를 정해서 감정과 해결 과정을 말하세요.

## 9. Movie, Music & Media

**메모리 포인트:** 취미 주제는 쉽게 짧아집니다. 작품 이름보다 “언제, 누구와, 어떤 기분으로 봤는지”를 말해야 자연스럽습니다.

### 3 Questions

1. What kind of movies or music do you like?
2. When do you usually watch movies or listen to music?
3. Tell me about a memorable movie or music experience.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** relaxing/funny/drama/music → helps me rest → fits my mood
- **Routine:** after work/weekend → phone or TV → family or alone → reduce stress
- **Experience:** watched with family/friend → expected simple fun → felt touched → talked after → remembered

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
I usually watch movies at home on weekends.
One evening, I watched a family movie without expecting much.
At first, I thought it would just be light and funny.
But near the end, the story became emotional, and my family started talking about our own memories.
After the movie, we stayed in the living room and talked for almost an hour.
That is why I remember the movie clearly, even though it was not a famous one.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

좋아하는 장르 나열보다, **그 경험이 나에게 어떤 대화를 만들었는지**를 말하세요.

## 10. Health & Exercise

**메모리 포인트:** Exercise는 Park/Walking과 결합하면 준비량을 줄일 수 있습니다. 헬스장보다 산책, 스트레칭, 컨디션 관리처럼 말하기 쉬운 소재가 안정적입니다.

### 3 Questions

1. What do you do to stay healthy?
2. How often do you exercise?
3. Tell me about a time when exercise helped you feel better.

### IH Answer Builder

- **Description:** simple routine → walking/stretching → not intense → realistic
- **Routine:** after work/weekend → short walk → water → sleep better
- **Experience:** tired/stressed → went for a walk → mood changed → slept well → kept the habit

### 60초 예시 흐름

```text
I do not do very intense exercise, but I try to walk regularly.
One day after work, I felt tired and stressed.
At first, I wanted to just lie down, but I decided to take a short walk near my home.
While I was walking, I listened to music and slowly felt better.
After I came back, I slept more easily than usual.
Since then, I have thought of walking as a simple way to manage my condition.
```

### IM2에서 IH로 바꾸는 포인트

운동 종류보다 **컨디션 변화**를 말하세요. “I was tired → I walked → I felt better”만 잡아도 60초 답변이 됩니다.

## 빠른 자기평가

- 질문을 듣고 5초 안에 **사건 하나**를 정했는가?
- 답변 중간에 `when suddenly`, `at first`, `so`, `fortunately`, `since then` 중 3개 이상을 썼는가?
- 마지막 문장이 단순 결론이 아니라 **느낌이나 변화**로 끝났는가?
- 같은 주제에서도 Description, Routine, Experience 답변이 서로 다르게 들리는가?
