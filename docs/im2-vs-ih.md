# IM2 vs IH

같은 질문이라도 답변 구조에 따라 등급 인상이 어렵거나 쉬워집니다.

## Question

Tell me about a memorable experience you had at a park.

<div class="script-grid">
  <div class="panel">
    <strong>IM2 Style</strong>
    <p>I went to a park last weekend.</p>
    <p>I went there with my family.</p>
    <p>There were many people.</p>
    <p>My son played there.</p>
    <p>We had a good time.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="panel">
    <strong>IH Style</strong>
    <p>I went to a park with my family last weekend.</p>
    <p>We were walking around when my son suddenly saw a big dog.</p>
    <p>He immediately ran toward it, so I was a little worried at first.</p>
    <p>Fortunately, the dog was very friendly.</p>
    <p>Its owner let my son pet the dog, and he was really excited.</p>
    <p>That small experience made the day memorable.</p>
  </div>
</div>

## 분석표

| 항목 | IM2 | IH |
|---|---|---|
| 문장 | 개별 문장 | 연결된 문장 |
| Story | 거의 없음 | 사건 흐름 있음 |
| 시제 | 단순 과거 반복 | 배경과 사건 구분 |
| 연결어 | and 중심 | when, so, fortunately |
| Detail | 일반적 | 반응과 결과 포함 |
| 기억 포인트 | 약함 | 마지막 이유 제시 |
